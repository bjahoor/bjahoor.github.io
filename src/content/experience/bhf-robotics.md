An autonomous weeding robot has to solve two problems at once: drive the rows without a
human, and tell a weed from a crop at speed. I worked on both ends.

On the software side I wrote the **ROS 2 autodrive** routine that navigates the field, and trained the
**deep-learning detection model** that classifies weed against crop from the camera feed. Getting this
wrong in one direction leaves weeds standing; getting it wrong in the other destroys the harvest.

On the hardware side I ran parts through the full cycle — CAD, **FEA in SolidWorks**, manufacturing
drawings, sourcing the fabrication, and physically installing the finished components. Being responsible
for a part from simulation through to bolting it on changes how carefully you simulate it.

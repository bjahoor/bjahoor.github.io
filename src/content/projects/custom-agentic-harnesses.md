Local LLM infrastructure running on a **Jetson AGX Xavier**, exposing OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible
endpoints from an optimized **llama.cpp** build.

On top of it runs a set of agents — **Hermes** supergents with **Claude Code** delegates — operating
autonomously on the local network.

Industrial control on a **Siemens PLC**, programmed in ladder logic to coordinate motors, sensors,
and button inputs.

Ladder logic looks archaic next to modern robotics software, and it runs most of the factory floor. It is
worth being fluent in the thing that is actually deployed, not just the thing that is interesting.

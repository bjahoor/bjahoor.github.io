A team-built **LEGO EV3** robot that reads the state of a Rubik's cube and solves it, programmed
in **RobotC** with a **Python** solving algorithm behind it.

The mechanism has to physically manipulate the cube without losing track of orientation — a dropped face is
an unrecoverable state error. The project followed a formal design process and was documented in a full
engineering report.
